# Future Improvements

## Planned Enhancements

Future improvements to the Group Policy environment include:

- Department-specific drive mapping
- Security Filtering
- WMI Filtering
- Windows Update management
- BitLocker deployment
- Microsoft Defender advanced configuration
- Windows Firewall rule management
- PowerShell-based Group Policy reporting
- Group Policy backup and restore
- Group Policy version control

---

## Future Integration

The completed Group Policy environment will integrate with future CyberLab projects including:

- DNS Administration
- DHCP Administration
- PowerShell IAM Administration
- Identity Governance
- Enterprise IT Operations

---

## Long-Term Goal

Continue expanding the enterprise Group Policy environment to reflect production Windows infrastructure commonly found in enterprise Active Directory environments.
