# CyberLab Enterprise Infrastructure Roadmap

## Vision

CyberLab Enterprise Infrastructure is a long-term enterprise Windows Server and Identity & Access Management portfolio designed to simulate the responsibilities of a Windows System Administrator and Identity & Access Management (IAM) Engineer.

The goal is to build, administer, secure, automate, and document a realistic enterprise environment while demonstrating practical skills used in production IT organizations.
